SwapMe Fox City

A Roblox tycoon to play with friends: SwapMe Fox City guide

Opens 11 Nov 2026, 18:00 Prague time (17:00 UTC).

What it is

SwapMe Fox City is a coin tycoon in a block-built Prague: Charles Bridge, the Orloj, the castle with a funicular, trams, ski runs and a club called EPIX. It is free and runs on phone, tablet and PC.

Your first minutes

You start with 100 Swaps. Follow the beam to your booth and open the Basic vault for 50. Place the coin in a booth slot and it earns Swaps every second.

Merge three, earn triple

Combine three matching coins into a golden coin that pays x3. It is the fastest early upgrade.

Trade with friends

Both players put coins in the trade window, both confirm, a 5-second countdown runs and the trade completes. Good for filling gaps in your collection.

Beyond the booth

Ride the trams and the funicular to the castle, try four ski runs, race karts or join the club. Fox guides in the city can walk you through the best spots.
