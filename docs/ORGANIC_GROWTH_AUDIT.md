## Video audit protocol (before diagnosing low views)
For every actual published video record:
- URL, publish date/time, duration, platform, format, title, caption, hashtags, cover, audio rights.
- Views, reach/impressions, average watch time, completion, 2s/3s hold, retention curve, shares, comments, follows, profile visits, outbound clicks where provided.
- Show first 3 seconds frame-by-frame, identify the gameplay payoff, CTA, subtitles, safe zones, and whether footage is real.
- Classify: strong hook / weak hook / unclear game / no payoff / mismatched audience / distribution limited / insufficient evidence.
- Compare at least 3 variations per creative concept; report medians, not just one viral outlier.
- Separate platform-reported causes from hypotheses; never infer a shadowban without platform evidence.
- Deliver prioritized fixes and 3 revised scripts per weak video.
